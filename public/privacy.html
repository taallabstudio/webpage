2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, password credentials in protected form, account identifiers, verification and security status, and sign-in method.
  • Profile and checkout information: name, phone number, billing/contact address fields, city, state, postal code and country. Checkout currently asks for these fields. If you choose “Save these details to my account”, we also store them in your customer profile.
  • Files and transfer information: files you upload or receive, file names, sizes, types, transfer messages, recipient details, download counts and expiry information.
  • Purchase information: products, order references, payment status, amounts, coupon/discount information and payment-provider identifiers. Card and bank payment credentials are handled by Razorpay, not stored by Taal Lab.
  • Connected-service information: if you use Google sign-in or connect Google Drive, we process the account information and permissions needed to provide those features. Google Drive access tokens are stored for the integration.
  • Technical and security information: session identifiers, request information and records needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

3. Why we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users and prevent abuse.
  • Transfer, store, deliver and expire files according to the transfer settings shown in the service.
  • Process orders, verify payments, provide downloads, send purchase confirmations and provide customer support.
  • Save customer details when you explicitly choose that option.
  • Operate connected Google features and maintain service security, reliability and legal records.
  • Respond to requests, complaints and legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We use service providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Cloudflare: website delivery, application hosting, database and related infrastructure.
  • Backblaze: storage and delivery of private transfer and digital-product files.
  • Razorpay: payment checkout and payment verification.
  • Resend: account verification, security and transactional email delivery.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Fonts resources used by the website, and (when connected) Google Drive integration.
  • Unsplash: photo search and download-attribution requests when you choose an Unsplash cover image in OCEAN. Your search terms and selected photo identifiers are sent to Unsplash.
  • Emoji API: emoji search and category requests when you use the emoji picker in OCEAN. Search terms are sent to the emoji service.

These providers process information under their own service terms and applicable privacy commitments. Google Fonts may receive technical connection information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests font files. Razorpay resources are loaded when you use checkout. Unsplash and the emoji service receive the search terms you submit to their respective pickers. We do not publish a claim that every provider has a particular certification or that every data transfer is covered by a specific contractual arrangement. Contact us if you need information about a provider used for a particular feature.

5. Cookies and local storage

The site uses a secure session cookie to keep you signed in. The digital shop also uses browser local storage to remember the contents of your shopping cart. These are used for core service functions. Payment checkout and Google sign-in may load resources from those providers when you choose to use those features. The site does not currently use an optional advertising or analytics cookie consent category. If you enable optional product and studio email updates in Account Settings, we record that choice separately from account and purchase emails. You can withdraw that optional preference at any time in Account Settings.

6. How long information is kept

We keep account information while an account remains active and as needed to operate and protect the service. Transfer files are configured to expire according to the expiry shown for each transfer; associated records may be retained for operational, security or dispute-handling purposes. Order and payment records may be retained for accounting, tax, fraud-prevention and legal requirements. Saved profile details remain until you update or request deletion of them, subject to applicable retention obligations. Transfer files are scheduled for deletion after the transfer expiry process. When storage deletion succeeds, associated file and transfer metadata is retained for up to 30 additional days and then purged by the cleanup process. If storage deletion fails, the cleanup process retries and the related metadata is retained until deletion can be confirmed. Account deletion requests are reviewed and processed after identity verification. Payment, tax and accounting records may be retained for the period required by law. We do not promise that every record is immediately erased when a transfer expires or an account is closed.

7. Your choices and requests

You can use Account Settings to download an export of account, transfer, consent and purchase information associated with your account, and to submit an account and personal-data deletion request. You may also write to us from the email address associated with the account. We may need to verify your identity. Some information may need to be retained where required for tax, accounting, payment disputes, security or other legal obligations. We will explain if a request cannot be fully completed.

Email: contact@taallab.in

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including protected account credentials, secure sessions and private file delivery. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a unique password and do not share private transfer or download links.

9. AI and automated processing

The current Taal Lab website and account service do not integrate a third-party generative-AI model provider for processing uploaded files or account content. Taal Lab does not use customer files or account content from this service to train AI models. If AI-powered features or providers are added later, we will review the data flow and update this Policy before enabling them.
