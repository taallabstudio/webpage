This Privacy Policy explains how Taal Lab (“Taal Lab”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you visit taallab.in, create an account, use file transfers, connect Google services, or purchase digital products.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, password credentials in protected form, account identifiers, verification and security status, and sign-in method.
  • Profile and checkout information: name and billing/contact address fields, city, state, postal code and country. Checkout also offers an optional phone-number field; you can leave it blank. If you choose “Save these details to my account”, the details you provide are stored in your customer profile.
  • Files and transfer information: files you upload or receive, file names, sizes, types, transfer messages, recipient details, download counts and expiry information.
  • Purchase information: products, order references, payment status, amounts, coupon/discount information and payment-provider identifiers. Card and bank payment credentials are handled by Razorpay, not stored by Taal Lab.
  • Connected-service information: if you use Google sign-in or connect Google Drive, we process the account information and permissions needed to provide those features. Google Drive access tokens are stored for the integration.
  • Technical and security information: session identifiers, request information and records needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.
  • Marketing preferences: whether you separately opted in to product news or marketing emails, the date of that choice, and any later unsubscribe record. Marketing consent is optional and is not required to create or use an account.

3. Why we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users and prevent abuse.
  • Transfer, store, deliver and expire files according to the transfer settings shown in the service.
  • Process orders, verify payments, provide downloads, send purchase confirmations and provide customer support.
  • Save customer details when you explicitly choose that option.
  • Operate connected Google features and maintain service security, reliability and legal records.
  • Respond to requests, complaints and legal obligations.
  • Send occasional product news or marketing emails only where you have separately opted in. You can withdraw that choice at any time.

4. Service providers

We use service providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Cloudflare: website delivery, application hosting, database and related infrastructure.
  • Backblaze: storage for digital-store product files and other app assets that use the configured B2 bucket.
  • Razorpay: payment checkout and payment verification.
  • Resend: account verification, security and transactional email delivery.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Fonts used to load website typography, and Google Drive storage and delivery for file transfers and connected Drive features. Loading Google Fonts may disclose technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to Google.

These providers process information under their own service terms and applicable privacy commitments. We do not publish a claim that every provider has a particular certification or that every data transfer is covered by a specific contractual arrangement. Contact us if you need information about a provider used for a particular feature.

5. Cookies, local storage and third-party requests

TaalLab uses a secure session cookie for sign-in (the application session expires after seven days) and browser local storage to remember the digital-shop cart. These are used for core service functions. The website requests font files from Google Fonts, which may disclose technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to Google. Razorpay resources are loaded when you open checkout, and Google authentication resources are contacted when you choose Google sign-in. We do not currently configure advertising pixels or optional analytics trackers on the website. The site displays a privacy and storage notice so visitors can read about these essential technologies. If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, they must be separately assessed and blocked until the required consent is obtained.

6. How long information is kept

  • Sign-in sessions: session cookies and corresponding server-side sessions expire after seven days; expired session records are removed by scheduled cleanup.
  • Temporary authentication records: email verification and two-step verification codes expire after ten minutes; password-reset links expire after thirty minutes; app hand-off codes expire after two minutes. Expired records are removed by scheduled cleanup.
  • File transfers: file objects are moved to Google Drive trash at the expiry time shown for the transfer; final removal follows Google's trash and backup lifecycle. Transfer and file metadata, including download-count and last-download information, is kept for up to one year after expiry, then purged after the move to provider trash has succeeded. If storage deletion fails, the related record is retained so deletion can be retried.
  • Booking anti-abuse and security records: rate-limit identifiers and relevant transfer/download metadata are retained for one year to support detection and investigation of unauthorised access. This does not extend the retention of uploaded file contents, which are scheduled for deletion at transfer expiry. Infrastructure logs held by service providers may follow their own retention schedules.
  • Account and OCEAN workspace information: retained while the account is active and until a verified deletion request is reviewed and processed, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security or dispute purposes.
  • Store customer profiles: retained while needed to provide account and checkout features, or until a deletion request is processed, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Order, payment and tax records: transaction details and associated processing records are retained for at least one year where required by applicable data-protection rules, and for longer where accounting, tax, payment-provider or dispute-resolution obligations require it. These records may not be erased when an account is deleted if retention is legally required.
  • Policy acceptance and privacy-request records: retained as needed to demonstrate the recorded acceptance or to document and handle the request, including related legal claims.
  • Marketing preference records: retained while needed to honour your choice and maintain an unsubscribe/suppression record so we do not send marketing emails after you opt out.

Scheduled cleanup runs hourly. Retention periods are not a promise of immediate erasure from provider backups or systems outside TaalLab's direct control. We review retention where legal requirements or service design change.

7. Your choices, access and deletion requests

If you have an account, open Account Settings to download an export of account, transfer, OCEAN and digital-store information associated with your account. The export excludes passwords, session secrets, OAuth refresh tokens, private storage keys and download tokens. You can also submit an account-deletion request from Account Settings. We record the request and review linked transfers, shared workspace content, purchases and any information that must be retained by law. Submitting a request does not immediately delete the account or its data. We may contact you to verify the request and will explain if information must be retained or cannot be deleted.

You can manage marketing preferences in Account Settings or use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Unsubscribing stops marketing messages only; essential account, security and purchase emails continue. Marketing consent is separate from accepting the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

You may also email contact@taallab.in from the address associated with your account to request access, correction or deletion. We may need to verify your identity.

The account export contains records held in TaalLab's databases, including file metadata but not uploaded file bytes or information held directly in connected third-party accounts. You can exercise rights relating to Google, Razorpay or other provider-held data through the relevant provider where applicable. Our subprocessor and service-provider list identifies the external services integrated with TaalLab.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including protected account credentials, secure sessions and private file delivery. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a unique password and do not share private transfer or download links.

9. Children

The service is not designed specifically for children and we do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate steps under applicable law.

10. AI and automated content

TaalLab does not currently provide AI content-generation features and does not use customer files, transfer contents or OCEAN workspace content to train or fine-tune AI models. If AI-assisted features or third-party AI processors are introduced, we will disclose the relevant processing and clearly identify AI-generated output before making those features available.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version. Questions, privacy requests and complaints can be sent to contact@taallab.in.
