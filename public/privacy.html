This Privacy Policy explains how Taal Lab (“Taal Lab”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you visit taallab.in, create an account, use file transfers, connect Google services, or purchase digital products.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, password credentials in protected form, account identifiers, verification and security status, and sign-in method.
  • Profile and checkout information: name, phone number, address fields, city, state, postal code and country. Checkout currently asks for these fields and stores them with the order. If you choose “Save these details to my account”, we also store them in your customer profile. We use these details for order records and order-related support; they are not used for promotional marketing without separate notice and permission.
  • Files and transfer information: files you upload or receive, file names, sizes, types, transfer messages, recipient details, download counts and expiry information.
  • Purchase information: products, order references, payment status, amounts, coupon/discount information and payment-provider identifiers. Card and bank payment credentials are handled by Razorpay, not stored by Taal Lab.
  • Connected-service information: if you use Google sign-in or connect Google Drive, we process the account information and permissions needed to provide those features. Google Drive access tokens are stored for the integration.
  • Technical and security information: session identifiers, request information and records needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

3. Why we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users and prevent abuse.
  • Transfer, store, deliver and expire files according to the transfer settings shown in the service.
  • Process orders, verify payments, provide downloads, send purchase confirmations and provide customer support.
  • Save customer details when you explicitly choose that option, and maintain the order information collected during checkout.
  • Operate connected Google features and maintain service security, reliability and legal records.
  • Respond to requests, complaints and legal obligations.

4. Service providers

We use service providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Cloudflare: website delivery, application hosting, database and related infrastructure.
  • Backblaze: storage and delivery of private transfer and digital-product files.
  • Razorpay: payment checkout and payment verification.
  • Resend: account verification, security and transactional email delivery.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Fonts, Google Drive and Google Calendar features. When you use these features, Google may process your account details, font requests or calendar/drive data as required for the feature.
  • Unsplash: OCEAN image search and image attribution/download tracking when you choose to search for or use Unsplash images. Search terms and photo identifiers may be sent to Unsplash.
  • Emoji API: OCEAN emoji search and category features. Search terms may be sent to the emoji data provider.

The checkout page loads Razorpay Checkout when you proceed to payment. The website loads font files from Google Fonts as an external resource. OCEAN can also contact Google Calendar, Unsplash and the emoji data provider when you use the corresponding integrations. These providers process information under their own service terms and applicable privacy commitments. We do not publish a claim that every provider has a particular certification or that every data transfer is covered by a specific contractual arrangement. Contact us if you need information about a provider used for a particular feature.

5. Cookies and local storage

The site uses a secure session cookie to keep you signed in. The shop uses browser local storage for the shopping cart, and OCEAN uses local storage for functional preferences such as recent emoji selections. These are used for core service functions. The privacy notice can be dismissed and remembers that choice in local storage. Payment checkout and Google sign-in load provider resources when you choose those features. Our current code review did not identify advertising or analytics cookies; if optional tracking is introduced, it must be disclosed and blocked until the required consent is obtained.

6. How long information is kept

Transfer files expire at the time shown for each transfer (the current default is seven days). An hourly cleanup job attempts to remove expired files from storage. After successful file deletion, associated file and transfer metadata is retained for up to 90 days after expiry and then automatically purged. If a storage deletion fails, the file remains queued for retry rather than being treated as successfully deleted. Account and profile information is kept while the account is active and until a verified deletion request is processed, subject to legal and security exceptions. Sign-in sessions expire after seven days; expired sessions, verification/reset tokens and short-lived rate-limit records are removed by scheduled cleanup. Order and payment records may be retained for accounting, tax, payment-dispute, fraud-prevention and other legal requirements; these records are not automatically removed with an account. Saved checkout profile details remain until you update or request deletion of them, subject to applicable retention obligations.

7. Your choices and requests

Signed-in users can download an account-data export from Account Settings and submit an account deletion request there. You may also contact us to request access to or correction of personal information. We verify requests before acting. Account deletion is reviewed rather than immediate; some order, payment, security or dispute records may need to be retained where required by law. We will explain if a request cannot be fully completed.

Email: contact@taallab.in

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including protected account credentials, secure sessions and private file delivery. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a unique password and do not share private transfer or download links.

9. AI and automated processing

The current Taal Lab application does not integrate an AI model provider to analyse, generate content from, or train models on customer files or OCEAN workspace content. If an AI-powered feature is introduced, we will update this Policy and provide appropriate information and controls before using personal data for that feature.

10. Children

The service is not designed specifically for children and we do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate steps under applicable law.

11. Changes and contact

We currently send account, security, transfer and purchase-related service emails; we do not currently send promotional marketing email campaigns. If we introduce marketing email, we will request separate optional marketing permission and include an unsubscribe option in each marketing message. Transactional service emails are not marketing messages. We may update this Policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version. Questions, privacy requests and complaints can be sent to contact@taallab.in.
