This Privacy Policy explains how Taal Lab (“Taal Lab”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you visit taallab.in, create an account, use file transfers, connect Google services, or purchase digital products.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: email address, password credentials in protected form, account identifiers, verification and security status, and sign-in method.
  • Profile and checkout information: name is required for a purchase; phone number and billing address fields (address, city, state, postal code and country) are optional. If you choose “Save these details to my account”, any details you provide are also stored in your customer profile.
  • Files and transfer information: files you upload or receive, file names, sizes, types, transfer messages, recipient details, download counts and expiry information.
  • Purchase information: products, order references, payment status, amounts, coupon/discount information and payment-provider identifiers. Card and bank payment credentials are handled by Razorpay, not stored by Taal Lab.
  • Connected-service information: if you use Google sign-in or connect Google Drive, we process the account information and permissions needed to provide those features. Google Drive access tokens are stored for the integration.
  • Technical and security information: session identifiers, request information and records needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

3. Why we use information

  • Create and secure accounts, authenticate users and prevent abuse.
  • Transfer, store, deliver and expire files according to the transfer settings shown in the service.
  • Process orders, verify payments, provide downloads, send purchase confirmations and provide customer support.
  • Save customer details when you explicitly choose that option.
  • Operate connected Google features and maintain service security, reliability and legal records.
  • Respond to requests, complaints and legal obligations.

AI and model training: Taal Lab does not currently integrate a generative-AI feature that processes your uploaded files, transfer messages or OCEAN content, and Taal Lab does not use that content to train AI models. If an AI-powered feature is introduced, we will disclose the processing and obtain any consent or permission required before enabling it. This statement describes Taal Lab's current application use; it is not a certification of every independent provider's general practices.

4. Service providers

We use service providers to operate parts of the service. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Cloudflare: website delivery, application hosting, database and related infrastructure.
  • Backblaze: storage for digital-store product files and other app assets that use the configured B2 bucket.
  • Razorpay: payment checkout and payment verification.
  • Resend: account verification, security and transactional email delivery.
  • Google: Google sign-in, Google Fonts used to load website typography, and Google Drive storage and delivery for file transfers and connected Drive features. Loading Google Fonts may disclose technical request information such as your IP address and browser details to Google.

These providers process information under their own service terms and applicable privacy commitments. We do not publish a claim that every provider has a particular certification or that every data transfer is covered by a specific contractual arrangement. Contact us if you need information about a provider used for a particular feature.

5. Cookies and local storage

Taal Lab uses essential storage needed to provide the service: a secure session cookie to keep you signed in, browser local storage for your shopping cart, and a preference cookie to remember your privacy choices. These are not used for advertising. We do not currently use advertising or analytics trackers.

Google Fonts are optional external resources. They are not requested until you choose “Allow optional fonts” in the cookie controls. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time using “Cookie settings” on the site. If you choose essential-only storage, the site uses system font fallbacks instead. Google sign-in, Google Drive/Calendar, Unsplash search and Razorpay resources are contacted only when you choose to use the corresponding feature or transaction.

6. How long information is kept

  • Sessions: sign-in sessions expire after 7 days. Expired session records are removed by scheduled cleanup.
  • Short-lived security tokens: email verification codes and two-step verification challenges expire after 10 minutes; password reset links expire after 30 minutes; app sign-in exchange codes expire after 2 minutes. Expired records are removed by scheduled cleanup.
  • File transfers: files expire at the date/time shown for the transfer. Scheduled cleanup attempts permanent deletion from Google Drive when no other active transfer references the same stored file. If storage deletion fails, cleanup retries. Expired file and transfer metadata is purged after 30 days.
  • Abandoned checkout records: unpaid orders with no recorded payment identifier are removed after 30 days.
  • Completed purchases and payment records: records needed for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, chargebacks, customer support or other legal obligations are retained only for the period required for those purposes. They are not removed by the abandoned-checkout cleanup.
  • Account and saved profile information: retained while your account is active and until a verified deletion request is processed, subject to information that must be retained for legal or security reasons.
  • Privacy requests: request records are retained as needed to verify, process and document the request and any applicable legal obligations.

Scheduled cleanup runs hourly. Deletion from a provider can be affected by provider outages or legal holds; where deletion fails, we retain the necessary reference so cleanup can retry rather than claiming the data has already been removed.

7. Your choices and requests

Signed-in users can download a copy of the account information currently associated with their TaalLab account from Account Settings. The export includes available account, transfer, OCEAN, security-method metadata, policy-acceptance, profile and purchase information. It excludes passwords, authentication secrets, session identifiers, OAuth tokens, private storage keys and download tokens.

You can submit an account deletion request from Account Settings. The request is recorded and sent to Taal Lab for review. A request does not instantly erase data: we verify it and review shared transfers, OCEAN content, purchases and records that must be retained. We will delete or de-identify information that is no longer required, and explain any information that must remain. Marketing emails are sent only when you separately opt in. You can withdraw that choice in Account Settings or use the unsubscribe link included in a marketing email; this does not affect essential service messages.

You may also contact us from the email address associated with your account to request access, correction or deletion. We may ask for information to verify your identity. Email: contact@taallab.in.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including protected account credentials, secure sessions and private file delivery. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please use a unique password and do not share private transfer or download links.

9. Children

Taal Lab services are not offered to children under 18. Account registration requires an affirmative confirmation that the user is at least 18; we do not request a date of birth. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can restrict access and review the information for deletion in accordance with applicable law.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this Policy when the service or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version. Questions, privacy requests and complaints can be sent to contact@taallab.in.
