## When U.S. registration is relevant

The U.S. Copyright Office states that a service provider seeking the Section 512 safe-harbour protections for hosting, caching or linking/search functions must designate an agent, publish the agent's contact information on its website and register the designation in the Copyright Office's online DMCA Designated Agent Directory. The designation must be kept current and renewed at least every three years.

TaalLab offers user file storage and sharing. A qualified U.S. copyright lawyer should assess whether its service and U.S. activity fall within the relevant Section 512 categories and whether TaalLab intends to rely on the safe harbour. Registration is not a universal requirement for every website; it is a condition for the relevant safe-harbour route.

Official sources:
- U.S. Copyright Office Section 512 overview: https://www.copyright.gov/512/
- Designated Agent Directory: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/
- Directory FAQ, renewal and required information: https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/faq.html

The current U.S. Copyright Office FAQ states that the fee is $6 per designation, amendment or resubmission, and that a designation expires after three years unless renewed. Required information includes the service provider's full legal name, physical street address, alternate names, and designated-agent contact details.

## Owner/legal actions

1. Confirm whether TaalLab wants to seek Section 512 safe-harbour protection for its user file-transfer/storage features.
2. Confirm the full legal entity/person name, alternate names, physical address and a designated agent's name/title, mailing address, phone and email.
3. If counsel recommends registration, register through the official online directory and publish the exact registered agent details on the site.
4. Implement an internal notice workflow: intake, validation, timestamp, evidence preservation, prompt disablement where appropriate, uploader notice, counter-notice, restoration timing and repeat-infringer review.
5. Record the registration date and schedule renewal before the three-year expiry.

Do not submit a registration or publish a named agent without the owner's authorisation and confirmed legal details.
