# Marketing email consent and unsubscribe rules

- TaalLab currently sends account verification, sign-in/security, password-reset, transfer and purchase/service messages. These are transactional/service messages, not marketing campaigns.
- The account setting `marketing_preferences` is separate from account creation, Terms acceptance and Privacy acknowledgement. It is unchecked by default and optional.
- A future marketing sender must query only accounts with a recorded `consented_at` and no `withdrawn_at`.
- Every marketing email must include a visible unsubscribe link using the signed `unsubscribe_url` generated by the Worker and point to `/unsubscribe.html?token=...`.
- Every marketing email must also include RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers:
  - `List-Unsubscribe: <https://taallab.in/api/marketing/unsubscribe>` (the sender must include the per-recipient token in the POST body or use a token-specific endpoint URL).
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`
- The unsubscribe endpoint accepts a POST with the signed token and records withdrawal without requiring login. Do not unsubscribe on GET because mail-security scanners may automatically follow links.
- Never include marketing content in account/security/transactional messages as a way to bypass marketing consent.
- Do not build or enable a marketing campaign sender until it is wired to the preference check and unsubscribe token flow.
