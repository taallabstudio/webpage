## Required sign-off

For each provider, retain:
- the exact legal entity and account/product used by TaalLab;
- the applicable service terms and DPA/addendum version;
- date and evidence of acceptance or signature;
- processing purpose and data categories;
- hosting/processing locations and international-transfer mechanism, where applicable;
- retention, deletion, backup and incident-notification terms;
- any approved sub-processors used by that provider.

Do not mark a provider as **DPA verified** until the responsible owner has checked the actual account and retained evidence. Ask qualified counsel to confirm where a processor agreement or cross-border transfer mechanism is legally required.
